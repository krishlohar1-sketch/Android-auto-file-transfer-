Krish Auto File Transfer

Designed to make nearby file transfer as easy as possible.

How pairing works

1. Receiver taps Receive and shows a QR.
2. Sender taps Send and scans it.
3. Receiver scans the sender's reply QR once.
4. A direct WebRTC connection is created and files travel device-to-device.

Important

For the best result, keep both phones close and on the same Wi‑Fi/local network. Browser security may still ask for camera permission.
